# Caption vocabulary by style family

Ingredient banks for the caption. Open **only the sections routed by
[style-router.md](style-router.md)**; skip the rest — do not read all 18
sections for one request.

How to use a card:

- Pick 3–6 dimensions across style, mood, instruments, timbre, vocal,
  production, and structure hints. Combine dimensions; do not stack synonyms.
- Wording is material, not a checklist. The brief wins whenever a card
  conflicts with it.
- Write qualitative tempo/groove words in the caption; numeric BPM, key, and
  meter go to metadata parameters only.
- Rewrite the example to fit the brief instead of copying it wholesale.

### `east-asian-modern`
- **Terms**: Mandopop, C-pop, urban pop, electronic pop, trap-influenced R&B production
- **Mood**: bittersweet longing, polished melancholy, confident swagger, bright nostalgia
- **Palette**: programmed drums, sub-bass, clean electric guitar riffs, layered synth pads, plucky arpeggios
- **Vocal**: breathy female lead, smooth male tenor, rap-verse feature, stacked chorus harmonies
- **Structure cues**: hook-first choruses, rap or dance breaks, electronic drops
- **Example**: "Mandopop with trap-influenced R&B production, warm sub-bass and crisp programmed drums, breathy female vocal, moody verses opening into bright anthemic choruses"

### `east-asian-ballad-heritage`
- **Terms**: Mandopop ballad, guofeng pop, orchestral East Asian ballad, acoustic C-pop
- **Mood**: restrained sorrow, cinematic nostalgia, tender longing
- **Palette**: grand piano, sweeping strings, acoustic guitar, guzheng/dizi/pipa accents
- **Vocal**: clear pure soprano, storytelling mid-register, gentle breathiness
- **Structure cues**: sparse piano intro, string swell into the final chorus, quiet outro
- **Example**: "Emotive Mandopop ballad, piano and string orchestra with delicate guzheng accents, clear female vocal, intimate verse building to a soaring cinematic chorus"
